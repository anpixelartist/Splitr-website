How it works

Three steps, then you're even

Create a group

Name it, pick a currency, and share the invite link or code. Sign in with Google, or try it without an account first.

Add expenses

Who paid, how much, and who it's for. Edits are stamped with who changed what, and deletes come with a 5-second Undo.

Settle up

Follow the plan, record each payment, and the receiver confirms it. The balance drops to zero and everyone can see it.

Splitr does the maths, remembers who paid, and shows everyone exactly what's fair, so your group can get back to the good part.

Download

Install Splitr on any device

Splitr is an installable web app: no app store, no download size worth mentioning, always the latest version. It gets its own icon and opens full-screen like any other app.

  1. Open Splitr in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

On iOS 16.4 and later you can also add it from Chrome or Edge using the same Share menu.

  1. Open Splitr in Chrome (or Edge, Brave, Samsung Internet).
  2. Tap Install when the banner appears, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app / Add to Home screen.
  3. Find Splitr in your app drawer and open it like any other app.
  1. Open Splitr in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar (a monitor with a down arrow), then Install.
  3. Splitr opens in its own window and appears in your Start menu, Launchpad or app list.

On a Mac in Safari 17+: File → Add to Dock. Firefox can't install web apps, but Splitr works fine in a normal tab.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Splitr really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no premium tier. It runs on free hosting and the code is open source.

Do my friends need the app?

No. Add them to a group by name and track what they owe. If they join later, they tap "that's me" on their name and every balance carries over.

Is there an App Store or Play Store version?

Not right now. Splitr is a web app you install from the browser (see Install). It looks and works like a store app, updates itself, and takes up very little space.

Does Splitr move money?

No. Pay each other however you like (UPI, bank transfer, cash) and record the payment in Splitr. Splitr never sees card or bank details.

What happens if I lose signal?

Keep adding expenses. They're saved on your device and sync to the group automatically when you're back online.
