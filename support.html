Getting started

How do I install Splitr?

Open Splitr in your browser and add it to your home screen. On iPhone use Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android use Chrome's Install app. On a computer use the install icon in Chrome or Edge's address bar. Step-by-step: Install.

How do I invite people to a group?

Open the group and share its invite link, or tell people the 6-character code. They choose Join in Splitr and enter it. Anyone with the code can join, so share it only with the group.

A friend doesn't want to install anything

Add them by name when creating the group (or later). You can still include them in expenses and record their payments. If they join later, they tap "that's me" on their name and all their balances carry over. An admin can undo a wrong claim.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Expenses you add offline are saved on your device and sync automatically when you reconnect. Other members see them as soon as your phone is back online.

Accounts & sign-in

Guest account or Google: what's the difference?

A guest account ("continue without account") lives only on the device where you made it. Signing in with Google keeps the same data on every device, and you can recover it if you lose your phone. You can start as a guest and switch to Google later from Profile.

I signed in with Google, but my guest groups are gone

If the Google account you picked already had Splitr data, Splitr switches you to that account. Your guest groups aren't merged into it. That's what the warning before sign-in means. They stay on the old guest account, which can't be reached again from that device.

If the Google account was new to Splitr, your guest data upgrades in place and nothing is lost.

Tip: before switching, ask a friend with a Google account to join your guest groups, so the data stays with the group.

Sign-in with Google seems to do nothing
  • On Android the Google sign-in opens in a new tab. Wait for it to close itself, and don't press Back or switch tabs early.
  • If you see "This Google account is already used with Splitr… switch?", choose OK to switch. Cancel keeps you as a guest.
  • Allow pop-ups for Splitr, and try again in a normal (not private) window.
  • Still stuck? Report it with your phone model and browser.
How do I delete my data?

Admins can delete expenses or a whole group from the group screen. To have your account and profile removed, contact us. See Privacy.

AI Connector

How do I use Splitr from Claude or ChatGPT?

Add the connector address https://splitr-mcp.splitr-mcp.workers.dev/mcp as a custom connector, then sign in with Google. Step-by-step for each assistant: Connect it.

"Guest accounts can't be connected"

The connector needs a Google sign-in. In the Splitr app, open Profile → Sign in with Google, then connect again.

The connector won't add, or fails straight away

Make sure the address ends in /mcp. On Claude's free plan you can have only one custom connector, so remove another one first if you need to.

My assistant keeps asking me to reconnect

Your Google sign-in ended, for example after signing out everywhere. Click Connect and sign in again. Your Splitr data isn't affected.

Expenses & payments

Why does a payment say "pending"?

A payment counts toward balances once the receiver confirms it, so nobody can quietly mark themselves as paid. It counts straight away if the receiver recorded it themselves or is name-only. Otherwise it confirms automatically after 7 days. The receiver can reject it, and whoever recorded it can cancel it while it's pending.

Can I edit or delete an expense?

The person who added an expense, or a group admin, can edit it. It keeps its original date and shows who edited it. Admins can delete expenses, with 5 seconds to Undo. Deleting an expense never removes recorded payments, because those are real money that changed hands.

Why is someone's share one cent more than everyone else's?

Some amounts don't divide evenly (₹100 ÷ 3). Splitr shares out the leftover cents so the shares always add up to the exact total.

Can I change a group's currency?

Each group has one currency, chosen when it's created. Splitr doesn't convert between currencies. For a trip across countries, pick the currency most expenses are in, or make a group per currency.

Does Splitr send or receive money?

No. Pay each other however you like and record it in Splitr. We never ask for card or bank details.
